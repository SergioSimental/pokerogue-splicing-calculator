        What do the held items do?

        Each Pokémon gets its own box, because when they fuse everything the secondary holds moves onto the primary. Matching items add up to their limit. Vitamins can't go above the primary's IV in either box, since the fused Pokémon keeps the primary's IVs. Vitamins, Soul Dew and Macho Brace change the stat shown. Shuckle Juice and Old Gateau change the primary's own base stats, and the fusion then averages them with the other Pokémon's. Eviolite and the species items only apply in battle, so they add an arrow with the in-battle value.

        • Sweet Shuckle Juice Adds 10 to each of this Pokémon's own base stats (5 to HP). A second one changes nothing.
        • Foul Shuckle Juice Takes 15 off each of this Pokémon's own base stats (7.5 off HP, rounded down). A second one changes nothing.
        • Old Gateau Adds 20 to this Pokémon's lower of HP and Speed, lower of Atk and Sp. Atk, and lower of Def and Sp. Def (a tie goes to Speed, Sp. Atk and Sp. Def).
        • Eviolite Boosts Def and Sp. Def by half when the Pokémon can still evolve. For a fusion the full boost needs both Pokémon to be able to evolve, and only half of it applies if just one can. No effect on Gigantamaxed Pokémon.
